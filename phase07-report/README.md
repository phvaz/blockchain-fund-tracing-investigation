## Publication constraints

Fixed in Phase 00 and reaffirmed here:

- **Neutral, methodological title.** The subject is the method, not the actor.
- **No attribution asserted.** Third-party attributions cited as claims.
- **Nothing undocumented published.** Anything surfaced that is not already in public reporting is withheld and reviewed privately before any disclosure decision.
- **No natural person named or implicated.**
